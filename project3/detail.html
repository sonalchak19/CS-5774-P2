Broccoli Lemon Pasta

Microwave-only 15 min ★★★★☆ 4.2 (12 ratings)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pasta
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Microwave pasta with water for 8 minutes.
  2. Add broccoli and microwave for 3 more minutes.
  3. Drain, then stir in lemon juice and garlic.
  4. Season with salt and pepper, and serve.

Why students like it

This is a go-to when the semester gets busy: it only needs a microwave and one bowl, and it uses up broccoli and a lemon before they go bad in the mini-fridge.
